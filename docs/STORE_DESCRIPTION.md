## Subtitle

## Description

Keep your Mac awake with one click from the menu bar. Caffeinate-d helps during presentations, downloads, and other tasks that need your display or system to stay active.

Turn it on until you switch it off, or choose a 1, 2, 5, or 10 minute session. Stop a session at any time.

Features
• One-click control from the menu bar
• 1, 2, 5, and 10 minute timers
• Optional Launch at Login
• Native macOS power management

Caffeinate-d does not prevent sleep when a MacBook lid is closed. No account is needed, and the app does not collect or transmit personal data.
