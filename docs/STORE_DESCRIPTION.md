# Mac App Store Listing: Caffeinate-d

## App Name
Caffeinate-d - Keep Your Mac Awake

## Subtitle
A minimalist menu bar utility to prevent system sleep.

## Description
Keep your Mac awake with a single click. Caffeinate-d is a lightweight, transparent, and minimalist menu bar application designed for professionals who need their system to stay alert during long downloads, presentations, or render tasks.

Built with native macOS technologies, Caffeinate-d acts as a sleek wrapper for the powerful 'caffeinate' system command. No complex settings, no heavy background processes—just a simple toggle to keep your screen on and your disks spinning.

### Key Features:
- **Instant Toggle**: Switch between 'Awake' and 'Normal' modes directly from your menu bar.
- **Native Aesthetic**: Uses system-native emojis (🍵/☕) to seamlessly blend into your macOS environment.
- **Minimalist Footprint**: Runs as a background-only app (LSUIElement); no Dock clutter or unnecessary windows.
- **Transparent Logic**: High-performance, open-source Swift code that respects your system resources.
- **One-Click Support**: Integrated links for documentation and developer support.

### When to use Caffeinate-d:
- During long file transfers or backups.
- While presenting to an audience.
- When monitoring long-running scripts or compiles.
- To prevent your Mac from locking while you're away for just a moment.
