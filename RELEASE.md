## Caffeinate-d v0.1.0 (Alpha)

Keep your Mac awake with a single click. **Caffeinate-d** is a minimalist macOS menu bar utility designed for professionals who need their system to stay alert during long downloads, presentations, or rendering tasks.

This is the initial open-source release of the project.

### ✨ Key Features
- **Minimalist Tray Integration**: Toggles the system `caffeinate` command directly from your menu bar.
- **Emoji State Indicators**: ☕ (Active) / 🍵 (Resting) for instant visual feedback.
- **Background Only**: Runs with `LSUIElement`, keeping your Dock clutter-free.
- **High Performance**: Built with vanilla Swift/SwiftUI for zero impact on system resources.
- **About Window**: Integrated links for documentation and developer support.

### 🛠 Technical Specifications
- **macOS Version**: 14.0+ (Sonoma and later)
- **Engine**: Wrapper for native `/usr/bin/caffeinate`
- **Architecture**: Universal (Apple Silicon & Intel)

### 🚀 Installation
1. Download the `Caffeinate-d_v0.1.0.dmg` attached below.
2. Drag the app to your `/Applications` folder.
3. Launch and enjoy the focus.
